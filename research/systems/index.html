Evidence-Governed AI Systems ​

How can we turn unstructured information into useful, personalized knowledge artifacts while preserving evidence, authority, and the scarce attention of human experts?

This is my developing research program. It grew out of building software and exploring the limits of AI-assisted development. The articles distinguish implemented systems, documented design proposals, and hypotheses not yet tested in controlled studies. They are not peer-reviewed publications.

A connected research vision ​

From claims to navigable knowledge connects the full experience: evidence-backed claims as the canonical knowledge model; distinct page-navigation and evidentiary graphs; Wikipedia/Wikidata as attributed discovery sources; semantic search; dynamically rendered dossiers; and a decision-oriented intelligence briefing with original-citation drill-down.

Why UAP records are an unusually demanding testbed explains exactly what the case can and cannot demonstrate, what source acquisition remains to be done, and why a second independently labeled benchmark is essential.

The research in one view ​

  • Knowledge: acquire multimodal material, construct claims and relationships, and retain access to exact sources, contradictory evidence, and uncertainty.
  • Orchestration: use self-describing sensors, deterministic tools, local/frontier models, and authorized reviewers to process work appropriately.
  • Governance: distinguish observations from qualified knowledge; record what was approved, by whom, under which policy and evidence.
  • Economics: spend expensive human judgment on decisions where it changes the outcome; measure errors, recovery, compute, and review time.
  • Outcomes: generate reliable personalized educational, business, and media artifacts from qualified knowledge.

Implemented evidence versus proposals ​

Implemented evidence: Chunky Monkey (document processing), MetaVis (Swift/Metal pipeline, sensors and QA code), Ordo Executor (private research prototype) (composable ingestion and candidate-promotion contracts).

Design and investigation: Ordo (business appliance doctrine, governance and a runtime that its README says has not started), UAP Corpus (private research catalog) (38 curated metadata records and acquisition design; source files not yet downloaded).

Open research question: no controlled evaluation has yet established the reliability, navigability or economic advantage of the full proposed architecture. This site presents that as a hypothesis worth testing, not a result.
