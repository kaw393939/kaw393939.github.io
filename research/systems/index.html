Research/Article

Evidence-Governed AI Systems ​

I want to be able to hand someone a briefing and answer a simple question about any sentence in it. Where did that come from? The hard cases are the ones in which a citation exists but points to another summary, two apparent witnesses turn out to be one, or the reviewer who could settle the matter has fifty other decisions waiting.

This collection brings together working software, design papers, and proposed experiments. The essays are personal research notes. The linked repositories let you inspect which parts I have built.

A connected research vision ​

A reader who wants examples can start with a video editor’s tests. A researcher interested in evidence can follow one disputed claim through a proposed interface. For a deliberately difficult dataset, see what UAP archives can actually establish.

Essays and technical case studies ​

The MetaVis engineering case contains code, tests, and hardware notes from a working prototype. The interface paper shows how a reader might inspect an answer, locate its source, and ask why it changed. The question that connects the two is what a person should be able to verify before accepting an AI-assisted result.

Academic documents ​

The preliminary dissertation proposal sets out potential experiments, comparison methods, and measures. The literature and faculty reading map identifies earlier work that informs those choices. Both are discussion documents. The proposal awaits formal academic review.

Implemented evidence versus proposals ​

Chunky Monkey contains document processing and verification code. MetaVis contains a Swift/Metal pipeline and quality checks. A private Ordo Executor prototype implements ingestion and candidate-review contracts.

The Ordo project documents the design for a broader runtime. Its implementation remains planned. The catalog snapshot described here contains 38 metadata records and an acquisition plan. The source collection, labeled benchmark, and evaluation of the combined system are future work. Its reliability, usability, and cost advantages remain open questions.
