# Careers evidence method

The careers area is a public educational resource, not a statistical labor-market survey.

- Target up to 10 distinct employer requisitions per clearly defined role family. Preserve actual titles and distinguish functional grouping from exact-title matches.
- Prefer direct employer or employer-authorized ATS pages. Record URL, employer, title, check date, location, experience and compensation basis when explicit.
- Count each requisition once. Location clones and duplicate syndications are not independent jobs. Do not pad a small sample to reach 10.
- Record skill evidence, not skills inferred from titles. A mention is not necessarily a hard requirement; preserve required/preferred distinction where available.
- Report skills as `mentions / postings reviewed` within each family. Absence of a mention does not mean a skill is unnecessary. This is a purposive convenience sample, not an estimate of prevalence in the industry.
- Preserve uncertainty, inaccessible pages and smaller samples. Treat closed, index-only or unverified listings separately from confirmed readable current examples.
- Salary ranges are employer ranges for specific locations and seniority, not averages or graduate-income promises. Do not combine hourly, annualized internship, base salary and total compensation into a mean.
- Retain dated source data and update the existing `/ai-careers` destination. Recheck links/deadlines on each scheduled edition; no extra schedule is needed.
