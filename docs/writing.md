## From the classroom

### [The Tests Passed. Who Won the Game?](/teaching/when-ai-helps-write-the-code)

*Classroom field note · November 2026*

A Rock, Paper, Scissors project raises an awkward question for the person running the demo. Passing tests can tell you what the program did. Who decided what it was supposed to do? I use examples from three NJIT courses to show where an AI coach can help and when a student needs the next turn.

## Research essays

These working papers trace questions I’m investigating through software I’ve built and systems I’m still designing.

- [English as a software engineering abstraction](/research/systems/english-software-engineering) explores what happens when a written instruction becomes a specification another person or an AI can test.
