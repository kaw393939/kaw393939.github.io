## Publication rules

A testimonial, published self-report, software README, historical benchmark log, independent news article and controlled experimental result have different evidentiary value. The website should not silently promote one type into another.

Claims about student learning gains, broad commercial adoption, deployment maturity, original invention, research sponsorship, or approval by advisors **require new evidence before being asserted**.

For research on uncertain sources, a record establishing that someone **said** a proposition is not a demonstration that the underlying proposition is true.
