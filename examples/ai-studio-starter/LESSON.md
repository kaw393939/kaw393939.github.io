# Did the help desk change help visitors?

**Your task.** A campus help-desk manager wants to know what changed. These fictional waits, in minutes, come from five visits in each period. The records do not identify returning visitors.

| Before the change | After the change |
| --- | --- |
| 4, 5, 5, 6, 30 | 6, 6, 7, 7, 9 |

**Decide.** Would you lead with the average wait or the middle visitor’s wait? Say why that measure fits the manager’s question. Another measure is welcome if you can explain it.

**Try one change.** Suppose the original 30-minute wait had been 5 minutes. Predict what would happen to your conclusion, then check it. Your coach may explain a measure, sort values, or check the arithmetic. Paper, a calculator, or a partner works too.

**Tell the manager.** Give one sentence about these visits and one thing the numbers leave open. Point to the calculation you used. Use your worksheet or explain aloud.

**A new question.** The manager now wants to know whether visitors with only ten minutes between classes could use the desk. What would you check first? Choose before asking for a hint. Show someone how to check your answer.
