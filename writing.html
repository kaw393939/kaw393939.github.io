The Tests Passed. Who Won the Game? ​

Classroom field note · November 2026

A Rock, Paper, Scissors project raises an awkward question for the person running the demo. Passing tests can tell you what the program did. Who decided what it was supposed to do? I use examples from three NJIT courses to show where an AI coach can help and when a student needs the next turn.
