A useful first reading session ​

  1. Read our system overview to identify the boundaries we are evaluating.
  2. Study similar projects and record one useful idea and one important difference for each serious candidate.
  3. Follow the concept guide for unfamiliar mechanisms.
  4. Examine implementation resources at the actual repository and release level.
  5. Check recent developments for maintenance status, prerelease features, and changed assumptions.
  6. Turn the comparison into a bounded decision record and an observable validation question.

From research to a portfolio ​

Save the question, original sources, alternatives, decision, and a planned check. During construction, connect that decision to a real change. During testing, connect it to actual results and limitations. These records become credible case-study evidence for the career portfolio. The IS373 assignment roadmap shows what belongs at each course milestone.

Release dates and document-update dates are labeled separately. Linked projects and their claims are reference material, not proof that our proposed system is faster or that a dependency satisfies our requirements.
