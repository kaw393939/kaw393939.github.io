ONE COMPLETE RAG EXAMPLEFictional

Can I bring a friend?

  1. 01 · QUESTION

    “Can I bring a friend to the club?”

  2. 02 · RETRIEVED PASSAGE

    “Members may bring one guest. Register your guest before the meeting.”

    Retrieved from Club handbook · §2
  3. 03 · EXAMPLE ANSWER

    Yes, you can bring one friend. Register them before the meeting. [1]

  4. 04 · CHECK THE SOURCE

    [1] Club handbook · §2: Guests

    Members may bring one guest. Register your guest before the meeting.

    This is the complete fictional source for this example. It says nothing about guest fees, so the answer should not invent one.
Retrieval finds evidence. Generation writes the answer.This written example is not a live chatbot. Follow the citation yourself, then ask what the handbook tells you about guest fees.
